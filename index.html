Walk-through and practice notes

First walk the roles, then rehearse the grounded T/N combination along the byline. Add C/F/L, then a defender who can follow or hold, then a second who can press. Reward a good recycle. No holding, illegal screens or blocking the goalkeeper. Defenders are illustrative; the board does not predict a successful finish.

Receiving directly from a corner has an offside exception; the return and later attacking touches do not. At N's layoff, T is farther from the goal line than the ball. C/F/L reset before T's later delivery. Check actual defenders and ball position in practice.

Use grounded service and empty target zones for most repetitions. Follow your club's current age-specific heading guidance.
